## How to write a YouTube Shorts script prompt

Use the same structure every time (ROLE / CONTEXT / TASK / CONSTRAINTS / OUTPUT):

| Part | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **ROLE** | Who the AI acts as, so the tone and judgment fit short-form video. |
| **CONTEXT** | Your niche, audience and topic, filled in by you. |
| **TASK** | The one script format you want written. |
| **CONSTRAINTS** | Length, word count, faceless rules and what the AI must not invent. |
| **OUTPUT** | 3 hook options plus a timed table with time, on screen text, voiceover, visual and sound. |

## 8 free YouTube Shorts hooks and script prompts

### Nobody Tells You
*Hooks: curiosity*

Use when: You are sharing a detail that is rarely covered in your niche.

```text
Nobody tells you this about [TOPIC].
```

Tip: Make sure the next line delivers a real, specific detail.

### Stop Doing This
*Hooks: mistakes and myths*

Use when: A popular habit works against a goal.

```text
Stop doing [COMMON_ACTION] if you want [GOAL].
```

Tip: Name a specific action, not a vague habit.

### One Change
*Hooks: before and after*

Use when: A single change produced a visible difference.

```text
Before: [OLD_STATE]. After: [NEW_STATE]. One change.
```

Tip: Split the screen so both states appear in frame one.

### Tested Both
*Hooks: comparisons*

Use when: You personally tried two options.

```text
[OPTION_A] or [OPTION_B]? I tested both.
```

Tip: Name your test criteria in the next line.

### In N Steps
*Hooks: how-to*

Use when: A process fits a short list.

```text
How to [RESULT] in [NUMBER] steps.
```

Tip: Keep the number at 3 to 5 for Shorts.

### Almost Happened
*Hooks: stories*

Use when: You avoided a bad outcome.

```text
I almost [BAD_OUTCOME] until I tried [ACTION].
```

Tip: Keep the stakes real and modest.

### Should Know
*Hooks: lists*

Use when: You teach basics to a specific audience.

```text
[NUMBER] [THINGS] every [AUDIENCE] should know.
```

Tip: Keep the number at 5 or fewer.

### Listicle
*Faceless script briefs*

Use when: You want a fast numbered list.

```text
ROLE: You are a short-form scriptwriter for faceless videos.
CONTEXT: Niche: [NICHE]. Audience: [AUDIENCE]. Topic: [TOPIC].
TASK: Write a listicle with [NUMBER] items.
CONSTRAINTS: 20 to 35 seconds. 60 to 85 spoken words. No face on camera. Each item gets one sentence and one visual. Do not invent statistics.
OUTPUT: 3 hook options (max 12 words each). Then a table with columns: Time | On screen text | Voiceover | Visual | Sound. Finish with a one-line call to action.
```

Tip: Set [NUMBER] to 3 or 5 for the best pacing.

## How to use these briefs

1. Pick a brief or hook, then replace every [PLACEHOLDER] with your real topic, niche and facts.
2. Paste the full brief into Claude, ChatGPT or any chat AI as one message.
3. Use the 3 hook options the AI returns, or fill in a template from the hook sections yourself.
4. Only give the AI numbers, results and quotes that are real. Leave a placeholder blank if you have none.
5. Read the voiceover aloud once and trim any line you stumble on before you record.
6. Ask for a rewrite of one row at a time instead of the whole table.
