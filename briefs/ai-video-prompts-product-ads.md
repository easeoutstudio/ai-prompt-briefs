## How to write an AI product video prompt

Use the same structure every time (Shot brief (8 lines)):

| Part | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **SHOT** | The one moment the clip shows, in a single sentence. |
| **SUBJECT** | The product and what surrounds it, with color and material. |
| **CAMERA** | Lens in mm, angle, and how the camera moves. |
| **MOTION** | What moves in the frame, how fast, and in what order. |
| **LIGHT** | Light direction, softness and color temperature. |
| **STYLE** | The look: color palette, finish and mood. |
| **DURATION** | Aspect ratio and clip length in seconds. |
| **AVOID** | Things the model must not do or add. |

### Slow orbit on a pedestal
*Hero product shots*

Use when: You need a premium main clip for a product page or ad opener.

```text
SHOT: Slow 180 degree orbit around the product on a pedestal.
SUBJECT: [PRODUCT] on a round [PEDESTAL_COLOR] pedestal, plain [BACKGROUND_COLOR] backdrop, product matches the reference photo exactly.
CAMERA: 50mm lens, eye level, smooth orbit left to right, product stays centered.
MOTION: Camera moves only. Product stays still. Speed is constant.
LIGHT: Soft key light from the upper left, gentle rim light, 5500K.
STYLE: Clean studio commercial, [BRAND_COLORS] palette, matte surfaces.
DURATION: 9:16, 8 seconds.
AVOID: Warped shape, changed logo, extra objects, text overlays, flicker.
```

Tip: Cut the orbit to 90 degrees by writing '90 degree orbit' if the product's back side looks wrong.

### Liquid pour
*Macro and texture*

Use when: You sell a drink, oil, sauce or liquid product and want a pour close-up.

```text
SHOT: Macro of [LIQUID] pouring into a [CONTAINER] in slow motion.
SUBJECT: [LIQUID] in [LIQUID_COLOR], clear [CONTAINER], [PRODUCT] bottle visible but soft in the background.
CAMERA: 100mm macro lens, low angle, locked.
MOTION: Slow motion pour at 25 percent speed, liquid fills 1/3 of the container.
LIGHT: Backlight from behind with a soft front fill, 5000K.
STYLE: Glossy, rich color, shallow depth of field.
DURATION: 9:16, 5 seconds.
AVOID: Spills, bubbles that look like glitches, logo text appearing, hands.
```

Tip: Name the thickness, such as 'thick like honey' or 'thin like water', to match the motion.

### Unboxing hands
*Lifestyle and hands*

Use when: You want a first-reveal clip for a product with nice packaging.

```text
SHOT: Two hands lift the lid off a box and reveal the product.
SUBJECT: A [BOX_COLOR] box with [PRODUCT] inside on a [SURFACE_COLOR] table, hands with [SKIN_TONE_AND_SLEEVE] visible from the wrists.
CAMERA: 35mm lens, top-down at 70 degrees, locked.
MOTION: Hands lift the lid slowly over 2 seconds, set it aside, and stop. Product stays inside.
LIGHT: Soft window light from the left, 5000K.
STYLE: Natural, tidy, warm neutral palette.
DURATION: 9:16, 8 seconds.
AVOID: Extra fingers, hands touching the product, changed packaging, text.
```

Tip: Say 'only the lid moves' if the hands start grabbing the product.

### Burger build
*Food and drink*

Use when: You need a stacked-build clip for a burger or sandwich.

```text
SHOT: A burger assembles layer by layer on a plate.
SUBJECT: A burger with bottom bun, patty, [CHEESE_TYPE], [TOPPINGS] and top bun, on a [PLATE_COLOR] plate.
CAMERA: 50mm lens, eye level, locked.
MOTION: Each layer drops in with a small settle, 5 layers over 5 seconds, ending with the top bun.
LIGHT: Warm side light, 3500K, soft shadows.
STYLE: Juicy, bright, saturated, dark [BACKGROUND_COLOR] backdrop.
DURATION: 9:16, 8 seconds.
AVOID: Floating pieces, odd sauce drips, extra layers, text.
```

Tip: State the exact layer count so the stack does not grow.

### Serum drop
*Beauty and skincare*

Use when: You want a clean drop shot from a dropper bottle.

```text
SHOT: One drop forms on a dropper tip and falls.
SUBJECT: A glass dropper bottle of [SERUM_COLOR] serum, exact match to the reference photo, [SURFACE_COLOR] background.
CAMERA: 100mm macro lens, eye level, locked.
MOTION: A drop forms over 2 seconds and falls at 3 seconds in slow motion.
LIGHT: Soft backlight, 5500K, bright highlight on the drop.
STYLE: Clean, minimal, glassy, [BRAND_COLORS] palette.
DURATION: 9:16, 5 seconds.
AVOID: Changed label, bubbles, skin shown, claims text.
```

Tip: Name the serum thickness, such as 'light and watery', for the drop shape.

### Earbuds case open
*Tech and gadgets*

Use when: You sell earbuds, a small case or any hinged accessory.

```text
SHOT: The case lid opens and the earbuds show inside.
SUBJECT: A [CASE_COLOR] earbud case with earbuds inside, exact match to the reference photo, [SURFACE_COLOR] surface.
CAMERA: 85mm lens, 30 degree angle, slow push-in 10 percent.
MOTION: Lid opens 90 degrees over 2 seconds, a small status light turns on at 3 seconds.
LIGHT: Soft top light with a [ACCENT_COLOR] edge light, 5600K.
STYLE: Clean, precise, tech look.
DURATION: 9:16, 5 seconds.
AVOID: Extra earbuds, changed hinge, floating parts, text.
```

Tip: Say 'hinge at the back, opens upward' to avoid wrong opening.

### Sneaker turn
*Fashion and accessories*

Use when: You sell shoes and want a clean 360 look.

```text
SHOT: A sneaker turns slowly on a low platform.
SUBJECT: A [SNEAKER_COLOR] sneaker, exact match to the reference photo, [BACKGROUND_COLOR] backdrop.
CAMERA: 50mm lens, low angle, locked.
MOTION: Sneaker rotates 360 degrees clockwise over 8 seconds.
LIGHT: Two soft side lights, 5500K, a small soft shadow below.
STYLE: Clean, bold color blocking, sharp edges.
DURATION: 9:16, 8 seconds.
AVOID: Changed laces, merged soles, extra shoes, text.
```

Tip: Say 'both laces stay tied and in place' to avoid morphing.

### Match cut between colors
*Transitions and loops*

Use when: You sell a product in several colors and want a quick color change.

```text
SHOT: The product holds still while the scene color changes from [COLOR_1] to [COLOR_2].
SUBJECT: [PRODUCT] in [COLOR_1] changing to [COLOR_2], same position, same angle, exact match to the reference photos.
CAMERA: 50mm lens, eye level, locked.
MOTION: A color wipe moves left to right over 1 second at the 2 second mark. Product shape does not change.
LIGHT: Soft studio light, 5500K, constant.
STYLE: Clean, graphic, flat backdrop.
DURATION: 9:16, 5 seconds.
AVOID: Shape change, camera movement, added text, flicker.
```

Tip: Use image-to-video with the color 1 photo and ask for color 2 in the prompt.

## How to use these briefs

1. Start from a real, sharp photo of your product and use an image-to-video mode. The product stays accurate and the brief only has to direct the motion.
2. Replace every [PLACEHOLDER] with your own details, then paste the brief as one block.
3. Keep the aspect ratio (9:16 by default) and the clip length (5 or 8 seconds) in the DURATION line. Change them only if your tool needs it.
4. Generate 3 to 4 takes per brief and keep the best one. Change one line at a time between takes.
5. If the result is off, open the Fix-it follow-ups and add the matching line to your brief.
6. Cut the clips together in any editor. Add real logos, prices and text there, not in the AI model.
