## How to write an AI product photo prompt

Use the same structure every time (SUBJECT / SURFACE / LIGHT / CAMERA / COMPOSITION / STYLE / AVOID):

| Part | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **SUBJECT** | What the product is, its real color, material and size. |
| **SURFACE** | What the product sits on or in front of. |
| **LIGHT** | Where light comes from, how hard or soft it is, and its color. |
| **CAMERA** | Lens in mm, angle and depth of field. |
| **COMPOSITION** | Where the product sits in the frame and how much space surrounds it. |
| **STYLE** | The overall look, such as clean catalog or warm editorial. |
| **AVOID** | Things the image must not contain. |
| **ASPECT RATIO** | The frame shape, such as 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9. |

## 7 free AI product photo prompts

### White seamless
*Studio packshots*

Use when: You need a marketplace main image on pure white.

```text
SUBJECT: [PRODUCT], [REAL_COLOR], [MATERIAL], true to the reference photo
SURFACE: white seamless backdrop curving into the floor, no visible horizon
LIGHT: large softbox from front left, white fill card on right, soft contact shadow under product
CAMERA: 85mm lens, f/8, eye level, whole product sharp
COMPOSITION: product centered, filling 70 percent of frame height, even margin on all sides
STYLE: clean catalog photo, neutral color, true-to-life
AVOID: extra props, text, invented features, altered logo, color cast on the white
ASPECT RATIO: 1:1
```

Tip: Add 'background pure white, RGB 255' if the backdrop looks gray.

### Kitchen counter
*Lifestyle scenes*

Use when: Your product belongs in a home kitchen.

```text
SUBJECT: [PRODUCT], [REAL_COLOR], true to the reference photo, at real size
SURFACE: light [COUNTER_MATERIAL] kitchen counter with a blurred [BACKSPLASH_COLOR] backsplash
LIGHT: soft morning window light from the left, warm 4500K tone
CAMERA: 50mm lens, f/2.8, eye level, product sharp, background soft
COMPOSITION: product on the right third, 2 or 3 small props such as [PROP_LIST] blurred behind, space on the left
STYLE: natural, lived-in, editorial
AVOID: clutter, brand logos on props, text, invented product features
ASPECT RATIO: 4:5
```

Tip: Cut props to one if the product gets lost.

### Morning routine
*Flat lays*

Use when: You sell items used at the start of the day.

```text
SUBJECT: [PRODUCT], [REAL_COLOR], true to the reference photo, plus a mug, a folded linen napkin and a small plant sprig
SURFACE: [SURFACE_MATERIAL] tabletop
LIGHT: soft morning window light from the top left, long gentle shadows
CAMERA: 35mm lens, f/6.3, directly overhead
COMPOSITION: product slightly off-center, props in a loose triangle, 15 percent empty margin
STYLE: calm, warm, editorial
AVOID: crowded layout, readable labels on props, invented features
ASPECT RATIO: 4:5
```

Tip: Remove one prop at a time until the product reads instantly.

### Bottle with condensation
*Food and drink*

Use when: You sell cold drinks.

```text
SUBJECT: [BOTTLE_TYPE] of [DRINK_NAME], [LIQUID_COLOR], with the real label from the reference photo, covered in fine water droplets
SURFACE: wet dark slate with a few ice cubes around the base
LIGHT: strong backlight from upper right to light the droplets and liquid, soft fill from left
CAMERA: 100mm lens, f/4, eye level, droplets sharp
COMPOSITION: bottle centered, tall space above, ice at the bottom
STYLE: crisp, cold, high contrast
AVOID: changed label, wrong liquid color, fake brand names, invented features
ASPECT RATIO: 4:5
```

Tip: Add 'fewer droplets' if the label gets covered.

### Serum with water ripples
*Beauty and jewelry*

Use when: You sell skincare in a dropper or pump bottle.

```text
SUBJECT: [BOTTLE_TYPE] of [SERUM_NAME], [GLASS_COLOR], real label from the reference photo
SURFACE: shallow clear water with soft concentric ripples, [BACKDROP_COLOR] backdrop
LIGHT: soft top light with backlight to catch the ripples, gentle highlight on the glass
CAMERA: 100mm lens, f/5.6, slightly above eye level
COMPOSITION: bottle centered with its base in the water, ripples extending evenly, open space above
STYLE: fresh, clinical-clean, translucent
AVOID: changed label, distorted bottle, claims, extra products, invented features
ASPECT RATIO: 4:5
```

Tip: Request 'small ripples' if the water overwhelms the bottle.

### Headphones on desk
*Tech and home*

Use when: You sell headphones or audio gear.

```text
SUBJECT: [HEADPHONE_TYPE] headphones in [REAL_COLOR], true to the reference photo
SURFACE: [WOOD_TYPE] desk with a notebook and a small plant
LIGHT: soft window light from the left, subtle rim highlight
CAMERA: 50mm lens, f/3.5, 25 degrees above
COMPOSITION: headphones centered, band curved naturally, blurred monitor behind with the screen off
STYLE: clean, modern, calm
AVOID: extra buttons, changed shape, readable brand marks, invented features
ASPECT RATIO: 3:2
```

Tip: Name the exact ear cup shape in your brief.

### Black Friday banner with empty space for text
*Sale banners and seasonal*

Use when: You need a dark, bold hero for a sale page.

```text
SUBJECT: [PRODUCT], [REAL_COLOR], true to the reference photo, placed on the right third
SURFACE: dark [SURFACE_COLOR] surface with a soft reflection
LIGHT: dramatic side light from the left, rim highlight on the product, deep shadows
CAMERA: 85mm lens, f/5.6, slightly low angle
COMPOSITION: product in the right third, the left 55 percent a clean empty dark area for headline text
STYLE: bold, dark, premium
AVOID: any text, letters, numbers, price tags, fake urgency graphics, invented features
ASPECT RATIO: 16:9
```

Tip: Keep the empty area free of gradients so text stays readable.

## How to use these briefs

1. Pick the brief closest to your shot and replace every [PLACEHOLDER] with your real product details.
2. If your tool accepts a reference image, upload a clear photo of your actual product and tell the tool to match it.
3. Describe only features your product really has. Never ask for details, colors or finishes it does not offer.
4. Generate 4 options, keep the best one, then use a fix-it follow-up if something is off.
5. For banners, leave the empty space empty and add text later in a design tool.
